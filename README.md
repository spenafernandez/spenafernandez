# Simón Peña-Fernández

**Full Professor of Journalism | University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU)**

Researcher in **Artificial Intelligence, Journalism, Digital Media, Disinformation, Social Media and Political Communication**.

## About me

I am Full Professor of Journalism at the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU), where I conduct research on the intersection of **artificial intelligence, journalism, disinformation and digital media ecosystems**.

My current research focuses particularly on the impact of artificial intelligence and algorithms on news media, journalists and audiences; the adoption of generative AI in journalism; disinformation and hate speech; algorithmic polarisation; and news consumption on social media.

Since 2012, I have published more than one hundred scholarly articles and have participated in around thirty publicly funded competitive research projects. I have led national and university-funded research projects on artificial intelligence, digital journalism, disinformation, polarisation and social media, and coordinated the CIBERCOM research network on online media and digital communication.

My research also has a strong international dimension, including participation in European research projects and international research networks in the fields of artificial intelligence, journalism and digital communication.

## Research expertise

My research expertise includes:

**Artificial intelligence in journalism** — adoption and impact of AI and generative AI in news organisations, professional practices and journalistic production.

**AI and media audiences** — audience perceptions of AI-generated and AI-assisted news content.

**Artificial intelligence and disinformation** — risks, professional perceptions and potential responses to AI-enabled disinformation.

**Algorithmic polarisation** — relationships between recommendation systems, social media, political communication and polarisation.

**Journalism and social media** — transformation of news production, distribution and consumption on platforms including TikTok.

**Online harassment and hate speech** — digital hostility directed at journalists and other public communicators.

## International research

## Knowledge transfer and professional engagement
